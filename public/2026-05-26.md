## PRIORITY OPPORTUNITIES

### 1. [Precision Strike Missile (PrSM) Increment 4 Prototype Request for Solutions](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/765b64e5f4aa41ddaebe960e0b1e440e/view)
**Army ACC-RSA seeks Increment 4 prototype solutions for PrSM long-range precision fires.**

**Agency:** Army ACC Redstone Arsenal

**Competitive Relevance:** Direct competition space for Anduril's munitions portfolio (Barrage, Altius). Inc 4 likely extends range/capability beyond current Inc 2 (~500km). Raytheon/Lockheed incumbents vulnerable to software-defined architectures and rapid prototyping models Anduril specializes in. This is core lattice integration opportunity.

**Acquisition Shift:** Army continuing modular increment strategy for hypersonics/long-range fires—validates preference for evolutionary prototyping over monolithic programs. Prototype authority usage suggests OTA pathway likely.

### 2. [Resilient Open & Agile Avionics System & Technology Development (ROAASTD)](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/a16a43fad6e34c59b34411753fc6bdc4/view)
**AFRL seeks open architecture avionics for resilient, upgradeable aircraft systems.**

**Agency:** Air Force Research Laboratory

**Competitive Relevance:** Strategic for Anduril's Ghost/Fury autonomy stack. "Open & Agile" signals MOSA/CMOSS adoption—threatens legacy integrated avionics primes (Collins, BAE). Palantir's AIP could compete on data fusion layer. Validates Anduril's bet on hardware-agnostic software architectures.

**Acquisition Shift:** AFRL explicitly prioritizing vendor lock-in mitigation. This is the technical foundation for NGAD's digital ecosystem and CCA interoperability. Watch for Mission Systems integration teaming opportunities.

### 3. [RFI for USSOCOM Technical Experimentation (TE) 26-4: Signature Management and Defeat](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/0085814b2fa8448cabf0cecb914935d5/view)
**SOCOM seeks signature management technologies for contested environments.**

**Agency:** US Special Operations Command

**Competitive Relevance:** Directly relevant to Anduril's ISR denial and EW capabilities. SOCOM TE pathway is rapid—90-180 day field experiments. Signature defeat spans EMCON, acoustic, thermal, RF—Ghost platform differentiator. Anduril's edge in fielded autonomous systems and low-signature design gives advantage over traditional EW contractors.

**Acquisition Shift:** SOCOM continues using TE as acquisition on-ramp for non-traditional vendors. RFI structure suggests they're hunting for novel approaches, not refining requirements for incumbents.

### 4. [Little Rock AFB Counter sUAS System](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/1cea74f87e38487c98fbc0e3196da483/view)
**AMC requires base defense counter-UAS system for Little Rock AFB.**

**Agency:** Air Mobility Command, 19th Contracting Squadron

**Competitive Relevance:** Anduril Sentry integration opportunity at strategic airlift hub. AMC protecting C-130J fleet from drone threats—validates critical infrastructure C-UAS market expansion. Competing against Dedrone, D-Fend, Fortem. Installation awards often cascade to other bases (precedent setting).

**Acquisition Shift:** C-UAS moving from CENTCOM warfighting requirement to CONUS installation protection—indicates threat normalization and budget prioritization shift. AMC ownership (not AFIMSC) suggests operational command buy-in.

### 5. [LPD In-Service Modernization and Shipbuilding - SMALL BUSINESS-ONLY Sources Sought](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/d09c2875914a43379b432fa208f427b8/view)
**Navy seeks small business partners for LPD amphibious ship modernization and new construction support.**

**Agency:** NAVSEA Headquarters

**Competitive Relevance:** Surface fleet modernization pipeline visibility. LPD-17 class receiving sensor/C4ISR upgrades—potential integration points for Lattice or Ghost Shark support systems. Small business carve-out creates subcontracting pathways under primes (HII). Signals Navy willingness to disaggregate traditional ship integration contracts.

**Acquisition Shift:** NAVSEA experimenting with small business set-asides on major platforms—unusual for shipbuilding. Possible response to acquisition reform pressure or industrial base resilience mandates.

### 7. [Drones](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/11fe99ea872e48e29f7a2eb52a23ce2d/view)
**Fort Riley MICC seeks commercial drones for training/operations.**

**Agency:** Army Mission Installation Contracting Command

**Competitive Relevance:** Tactical unit procurement pattern data point. Title vagueness suggests small unit-level buy (company/battalion), likely Group 1/2 UAS. Tracking volume and frequency reveals ground force adoption velocity and spec convergence—informs Anduril's Anvil/smaller platform roadmap. Blue UAS compliance required—eliminates DJI, advantages US manufacturers.

**Acquisition Shift:** Installation-level contracting for UAS (not PEO-level) indicates normalization as basic equipment. Accelerating decentralization of drone procurement to unit level.
