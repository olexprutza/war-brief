## 4. Three Questions

1. **The Senate Armed Services Committee (SASC) drone combatant command provision passed 18–9.** Which service will lose budget share when a unified drone command controls its own requirements? Knowing the losers tells you who will fight the provision in conference — and which acquisition offices will accelerate buys before the reorganization locks in.

2. **Iran downed a U.S. Apache with a drone in the Strait of Hormuz.** The engagement proves rotary-wing survivability against cheap UAS threats is unsolved. Is there an active program of record — or an OTA vehicle — for counter-drone protection of low-and-slow U.S. aircraft operating in contested Gulf airspace, and is Anduril positioned on it?

3. **The SASC bill and Eurosatory are running simultaneously this week.** European allies are told U.S. hardware won't arrive until 2029–2030 at earliest. Does Anduril have a path to direct commercial or Foreign Military Sale (FMS) conversations with Poland, Germany, or the Baltic states that bypasses the current backlog — and what export licensing constraints would need to move first?
