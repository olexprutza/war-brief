## 4. Three Questions

1. **Space Force's commercial data integration problem is named and on the record — who owns the budget line to fix it?** Col. Trimailo flagged the acquisitions-to-operators gap publicly. Find out whether Space Systems Command's Commercial Space Office has an active solicitation or an Other Transaction Authority (OTA) vehicle that could be the entry point, and whether Lattice is already in conversations there.

2. **What does scrapping public weapons testing reports actually mean for how Congress oversees new-entrant programs?** The testing office cuts could reduce scrutiny on legacy systems while simultaneously removing a transparency signal that helped challengers argue incumbents were underperforming. Worth mapping which specific reports covered programs Anduril competes against — and whether the Hill staff who relied on those reports are now looking for alternative data sources.

3. **The NSA Bahrain attack is being used to argue for reconstituting a fixed base — who is making the counter-argument for distributed, hardened, autonomous base defense, and is Anduril in that conversation?** The op-ed frames the answer as "rebuild what was there." That framing, if it wins on the Hill, steers money toward construction rather than technology. Someone in BD should know whether NAVCENT (U.S. Naval Forces Central Command) has issued any requests for information on autonomous perimeter defense since the attack.
