## 4. Three Questions

1. The Army's NGCM cost cap is $150K per missile. Anduril's Roadrunner family sits above that threshold in most configurations. What would it cost to field a variant that meets the cap, and does the unit-economics math still work at the volumes the Army needs to replace what it burned in the Iran war?

2. European buyers are structurally moving toward ITAR-free procurement. Anduril builds in the US and sells through standard Foreign Military Sales (FMS) and direct commercial channels — both ITAR-encumbered. Which Anduril products, if any, could plausibly be licensed for European co-production, and has anyone war-gamed what that does to the margin?

3. The Space Force's optical inter-satellite laser link testing is tightly coupled to Golden Dome architecture. Where does Anduril's space portfolio — sensors, battle management software, Lattice integration — actually plug into that architecture today, and who in the Space Force is the right relationship to develop before the acquisition moves from RDT&E into a program of record?
