## 4. Three Questions

1. **Project Gatekeeper goes live October 23 — is Anduril's software already positioned to run through it?** The sandbox is designed to clear commercial software for Army networks in minutes. If Anduril's Lattice stack is not in the first wave, someone else's will be. Who inside the Army CIO's office owns the vendor onboarding list?

2. **The IFPC "shoot-off" is open to vendors beyond the Lockheed and Boeing-Anduril teams — who is coming, and does their presence reframe how Army scores the Boeing-Anduril bid?** An open demo lane can either validate the frontrunners or give the Army cover to split an award. Find out which vendors are accepting the invitation and what capability gaps they claim to fill.

3. **FASCOM is now a named command with a mandate to own autonomous systems concepts — who are the one or two people inside it who will actually write requirements?** Gen. LaNeve set the direction, but requirements get shaped at the colonel and GS-15 level. Mapping those names now, before solicitations appear, is the whole game.
