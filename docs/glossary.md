# Glossary

Defense acronyms and terms, kept short. Add to this file as new ones come up.

## Acquisition and contracting

- **DoW** — Department of War. Renamed from Department of Defense.
- **OTA** — Other Transaction Authority. A contracting mechanism that sits outside the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). Used to move faster and reach non-traditional vendors. Most often run through a consortium.
- **MTA** — Middle Tier of Acquisition. A pathway for rapid prototyping and rapid fielding of capabilities, with a target of completing in two to five years.
- **SWP** — Software Acquisition Pathway. A pathway purpose-built for software, replacing the older waterfall-style acquisition milestones.
- **FAR** — Federal Acquisition Regulation. The default rulebook for federal contracts.
- **DFARS** — Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement. The DoW addendum to the FAR.
- **JCIDS** — Joint Capabilities Integration and Development System. The requirements process.
- **PoR** — Program of Record. A program with approved funding in the budget.
- **CSO** — Commercial Solutions Opening. A solicitation style for commercial items.
- **BAA** — Broad Agency Announcement. A solicitation for research, often with multiple awards.
- **SBIR / STTR** — Small Business Innovation Research / Small Business Technology Transfer. Phased federal R&D funding for small businesses.
- **IDIQ** — Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity. A contract vehicle that lets the government order against it over time.

## Organizations and offices

- **OSD** — Office of the Secretary of Defense.
- **PEO** — Program Executive Office. An organization that runs a portfolio of programs.
- **PM** — Program Manager (or Program Manager's office). Runs an individual program under a PEO.
- **JCO** — Joint Counter-small UAS Office. The DoW lead for counter-UAS.
- **DIU** — Defense Innovation Unit. Connects commercial tech to DoW problems.
- **CDAO** — Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office.
- **DARPA** — Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency.
- **AFWERX** — Air Force innovation arm. Runs SBIR and other commercial outreach.
- **SOFWERX** — Same idea, for Special Operations Command (SOCOM).
- **NGA** — National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.
- **NRO** — National Reconnaissance Office.

## Technology

- **UAS** — Unmanned Aerial System. The aircraft plus ground control plus comms.
- **UAV** — Unmanned Aerial Vehicle. The aircraft itself.
- **C-UAS** — Counter-UAS. Systems that detect, track, and defeat hostile drones.
- **ISR** — Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance.
- **C2** — Command and Control.
- **C4ISR** — Command, Control, Communications, Computers, ISR.
- **EW** — Electronic Warfare.
- **PNT** — Positioning, Navigation, and Timing.
- **LEO / MEO / GEO** — Low / Medium / Geostationary Earth Orbit.

## Programs and initiatives

- **Replicator** — DoW initiative to field thousands of attritable autonomous systems on a fast timeline.
- **CCA** — Collaborative Combat Aircraft. Air Force program for unmanned wingmen.
- **JADC2** — Joint All-Domain Command and Control. The vision for connecting sensors and shooters across services.
- **ABMS** — Advanced Battle Management System. Air Force's piece of JADC2.
- **Project Maven** — DoW computer vision program, originally for full-motion video analysis. Run by NGA and contracted heavily to Palantir.
