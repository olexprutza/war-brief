# People to Track

Analysts, writers, and operators worth following. Update as the list shifts.

## Acquisition reform

- **Bill Greenwalt** — Former Deputy Under Secretary of Defense for Industrial Policy. Architect of much of the modern OTA framework. Writes at AEI and elsewhere.
- **Eric Lofgren** — Runs *Acquisition Talk* (podcast and Substack). Deep on acquisition process, budget structure, and reform.
- **Pete Modigliani** — MITRE acquisition fellow. Publishes on software acquisition pathways.

## Defense strategy and tech

- **Chris Brose** — Anduril Chief Strategy Officer. Author of *The Kill Chain*. Read his book if you have not.
- **Bryan Clark** — Hudson Institute. Naval and autonomous systems.
- **Bryan McGrath** — FerryBridge Group. Naval strategy.
- **Elbridge Colby** — Author of *The Strategy of Denial*. Sets the framing for the China-focused defense posture.
- **Trae Stephens** — Founders Fund, Anduril co-founder. Writes occasionally on defense tech investing.

## Trade press and analysis

- **Breaking Defense** — Sydney Freedberg, Theresa Hitchens, Lee Hudson. Daily coverage.
- **Defense News** — Stephen Losey, Courtney Albon, Joe Gould.
- **War on the Rocks** — Long-form analysis. Ryan Evans runs it.
- **The War Zone** (drive.com) — Tyler Rogoway, Joseph Trevithick. Strong on systems and platforms.
- **Aviation Week** — Steve Trimble, Brian Everstine.

## Newsletters worth a subscription

- *Acquisition Talk* (Lofgren)
- *Defense One* daily brief
- *Breaking Defense* daily brief
- *Politico Morning Defense*
- *Punchbowl News* for Hill-side coverage that touches defense

## Cultural and philosophical voices

- **Ross Douthat** — *New York Times* columnist. Read him when he writes on the defense industry, American statecraft, or the China question. Not a defense specialist, but his frame on American decline and renewal touches the same nerve Anduril is pressing.
- **Andy Crouch** — Writer on technology and human formation. His distinction between **tools** (which extend human capacity and require skill) and **devices** (which substitute for human capacity and dull it) is a useful frame for thinking about Anduril's product philosophy. Lattice and the autonomy stack are pitched as tools for the warfighter, not devices that replace him. Worth reading any time.

## Investors and analysts who shape narrative

- **Katherine Boyle** — a16z American Dynamism partner.
- **Delian Asparouhov** — Founders Fund, Varda. Pushes the defense-tech-as-startup framing.
- **Byron Callan** — Capital Alpha Partners. Equity analyst, deep on prime contractor financials.

Notes:
- This list reflects who is loud and influential as of 2026. Audit it every six months.
- Following someone is not endorsement of their views. The goal is awareness of the conversation.
