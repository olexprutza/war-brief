# Program Offices and PEOs

Focus: autonomous systems, counter-UAS, and the offices Anduril sells into.

---

## Joint and OSD-level

### Joint Counter-small UAS Office (JCO)
- **Lead:** Army Executive Agent
- **Mission:** Coordinate the joint approach to defeating small UAS (Groups 1–3).
- **Why it matters:** Sets requirements, runs demos, picks the systems that move into service-level programs.

### Defense Innovation Unit (DIU)
- **HQ:** Mountain View, CA, with offices in Boston, Austin, Chicago, DC.
- **Director:** Doug Beck
- **Mission:** Field commercial tech into DoW fast, using CSOs and OTAs.
- **Relevant portfolios:** Autonomy, AI/ML, Space, Cyber.

### Replicator Initiative
- **Owner:** Deputy Secretary of Defense, executed via DIU and the services.
- **Goal:** Field thousands of attritable autonomous systems on a two-year clock from announcement (Aug 2023).
- **Tranches:** Iteratively announced. Anduril has won pieces across air and surface.

### Chief Digital and AI Office (CDAO)
- **Mission:** Owns the enterprise AI strategy and data infrastructure for the DoW.
- **Relevance:** Project Maven sits adjacent. CDAO sets the AI policy guardrails everyone has to live with.

## Army

### PEO Aviation
- **HQ:** Redstone Arsenal, AL
- **Portfolio:** Manned and unmanned Army aviation. Future Vertical Lift, FTUAS (Future Tactical UAS), Gray Eagle.
- **Relevance:** FTUAS competitors include Textron, Griffon, Anduril, AeroVironment.

### PEO IEW&S (Intelligence, Electronic Warfare and Sensors)
- **HQ:** Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD
- **Portfolio:** TITAN, sensors, EW.
- **Relevance:** TITAN went to Palantir. Sensor work touches Anduril's Sentry and EW work.

### PEO Missiles and Space
- **HQ:** Redstone Arsenal, AL
- **Portfolio:** Air and missile defense, including C-UAS interceptors.

### Rapid Capabilities and Critical Technologies Office (RCCTO)
- **HQ:** Huntsville, AL
- **Mission:** Field prototype capabilities fast. Uses MTA pathway heavily.
- **Relevance:** Hypersonics, directed energy, counter-UAS prototypes.

## Air Force and Space Force

### Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)
- **HQ:** Wright-Patterson AFB, OH
- **Portfolio:** Most Air Force programs flow through AFLCMC PEOs (Fighters & Advanced Aircraft, ISR & SOF, etc.).

### Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)
- **HQ:** Wright-Patterson AFB, OH
- **Mission:** R&D and prototyping.
- **Relevance:** Skyborg, Golden Horde, CCA precursors.

### CCA Program Office
- **Lead:** Air Force, under PEO Fighters and Advanced Aircraft.
- **Increment 1:** Anduril (Fury) and General Atomics (YFQ-42A).
- **Why it matters:** Defines the unmanned wingman concept that drives a large slice of future Air Force autonomy spending.

### AFWERX
- **HQ:** Las Vegas, NV (with Austin and DC presence)
- **Mission:** SBIR/STTR pipeline for the Air Force. Front door for small companies.

### Space Development Agency (SDA)
- **Mission:** Proliferated LEO architecture (Transport Layer, Tracking Layer).
- **Relevance:** Tranche 1, 2, 3 awards going to a mix of L3Harris, Lockheed, Northrop, York, Sierra Space, and the launch primes.

### Space Systems Command (SSC)
- **HQ:** Los Angeles AFB, CA
- **Mission:** Acquisition arm of Space Force outside SDA's lane.

## Navy and Marine Corps

### PEO Unmanned and Small Combatants (PEO USC)
- **HQ:** Washington Navy Yard, DC
- **Portfolio:** USVs (unmanned surface vessels), LCS, small combatants.
- **Relevance:** Saronic, Saildrone, HavocAI, Anduril Dive-LD adjacent.

### PEO Aircraft Carriers / PEO Aviation also exist, less relevant to autonomy at the top level.

### Marine Corps Warfighting Lab (MCWL)
- **HQ:** Quantico, VA
- **Mission:** Experimentation arm. Force Design 2030 implementation.

---

## SOCOM

### USSOCOM Acquisition (SOF AT&L)
- **HQ:** MacDill AFB, FL
- **Why it matters:** Fast money, fewer hands in the pot, willingness to buy commercial. SOFWERX is the front door.

---

## How to read this list

Find the PEO that owns the problem, then the PM under it that runs the specific program. Programs of Record are where sustainment money lives. OTAs and MTAs are where the new entrants get a foothold before there is a PoR.
