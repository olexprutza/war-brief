## Anduril Industries

- **Founded:** 2017
- **HQ:** Costa Mesa, CA (with Arsenal-1 plant under construction in Pickaway County, OH)
- **CEO:** Brian Schimpf
- **Founders:** Palmer Luckey, Trae Stephens, Brian Schimpf, Matt Grimm, Joe Chen
- **Core platform:** Lattice (software backbone for autonomous systems and C2)

**Product lines:**
- Ghost (small VTOL UAS)
- Altius (loitering munitions)
- Anvil and Roadrunner (C-UAS interceptors)
- Sentry (surveillance towers)
- Dive-LD (autonomous undersea vehicle)
- Fury (CCA candidate)
- Pulsar (electronic warfare)
- Bolt (man-portable strike UAS)

**Notable wins:** CCA Increment 1 (with General Atomics), Replicator tranches, JADC2 work, Air Force counter-UAS.

---

## Palantir Technologies

- **Founded:** 2003
- **HQ:** Denver, CO
- **CEO:** Alex Karp
- **Core platforms:** Gotham (defense/intel), Foundry (commercial), AIP (AI Platform layer)

**DoW footprint:**
- Project Maven prime since 2024
- TITAN (Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node) prime for the Army
- ARES Army Vantage data platform
- Heavy across intel community (CIA, NSA, NGA)

**Read:** Palantir competes with Anduril more on the data/AI layer than on hardware. Lattice and AIP overlap. Watch for clashes on Replicator and on JADC2-adjacent contracts.

---

## SpaceX

- **Founded:** 2002
- **HQ:** Hawthorne, CA / Starbase, TX
- **CEO:** Elon Musk
- **Defense relevance:** Launch (Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Starship), Starlink/Starshield, SDA (Space Development Agency) tranches.

**Watch:**
- Starshield contracts with NRO and DoW
- National Security Space Launch (NSSL) Phase 3
- Starlink as a battlefield comms layer (Ukraine has been the demo)

---

## Kratos Defense & Security Solutions

- **Founded:** 1994 (current form after 2010 reorganization)
- **HQ:** San Diego, CA
- **CEO:** Eric DeMarco
- **Core lines:** Tactical UAS (Valkyrie, Mako, Firejet), target drones, satellite ground systems, hypersonics components, microelectronics.

**Watch:** XQ-58A Valkyrie has been a CCA reference design. Kratos plays a different game from Anduril: lower unit cost, mass production, often as a teammate or subcontractor.

---

## Amazon Project Kuiper

- **Status:** Constellation under deployment (over 3,200 LEO satellites planned)
- **Operator:** Amazon (Kuiper Systems LLC)
- **Defense angle:** Direct competitor to Starlink/Starshield for resilient comms. Kuiper Government Solutions stood up to chase DoW and IC business.

**Watch:** Whether DoW treats Kuiper as a genuine second source to break Starlink dependency, or whether the schedule slips and SpaceX keeps the lane.
